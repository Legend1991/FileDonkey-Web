1. The 14-day window

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of the date of your order, as shown on your Creem receipt. You do not need to give a reason. Requests made after that date are not refunded, except where the law requires it.

Write to support@filedonkey.app from the email address you used at checkout, or include your order number from the Creem receipt.

Refunds are paid by Creem to the original payment method, including any tax that was charged. We answer refund requests within 3 business days; how long the money then takes to reach you depends on your bank or card issuer. A refunded license stops being valid.

3. Your legal rights

This Policy is in addition to your rights under the consumer law of the country where you live, and to any rights you have under Creem's Buyer Terms. It does not limit either.
